An interactive dashboard for monitoring the performance of all 10 substations under TOMS Semtokha, featuring insights on line losses, system losses, bus losses, transformer performance, feeder-wise peak load, and total energy. With monthly data uploads, the platform supports efficient analysis, trend monitoring, and data-driven decision-making for substations and transmission lines.
